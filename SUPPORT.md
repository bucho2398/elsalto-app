# Getting help

- **Questions, ideas, how-to:** [Discussions](https://github.com/bucho2398/elsalto-app/discussions).
- **Something broken or a feature request:** [Issues](https://github.com/bucho2398/elsalto-app/issues/new/choose).
  Search first; someone may have reported it already.
- **A wall or route is wrong or missing:** [elsalto-data](https://github.com/bucho2398/elsalto-data/issues),
  where the wall data lives.
- **A security problem:** privately, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The app is maintained by one person in their free time, so answers can take a few days. Wetness and
sun times are estimates: always check the rock before you climb.
